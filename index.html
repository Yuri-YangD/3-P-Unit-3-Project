Scallion Pancakes

  • Time: ~30 mins
  • Serves:~Two people
Ingredients Quantity
Green onions Quantity #1
Flour 1/2 cup
Water 1/2 cup
Soybean paste 1 Ts
Sugar 1/2 Ts
Vegtable oil 3 Tbs

Step 1: Clean and wash the bunch of green onions.

Step 2: Cut the green onions into 5 inch long pieces.

Step 3: To create the batter, mix 1/2 cup of flour, 1/2 cup soybean paste, and 1/2 ts of sugar in a bowl. Mix the batter until you get a smooth consistency.

Step 4: Put a non-stick pan on the stove. Then, add 3tbs of vegtable oil onto the pan onces its heated.

Step 5: Layout the green onions onto the pan paraell to eachother, shaped like a rectangle.

Step 6: Pour the batter over the green onions evenly. *You can also add additional ingredients to the batter like seafood, meat, & extra veggies.

Step 7: As the pancake is cooking, press lightly onto it with a spatula to keep it in a nice shape.

Step 8: Wait until the bottom is golden and crispy then flip it over; continue the same process to the other side.

Step 9: Plate the pancakes and you're all done!
